Gemma’s Gems support

Answers to common questions about purchases, adverts and restoring your game.

Contact support

Email support@ympl.co.uk. Please include your device model, operating-system version and a short description of what happened.

Restore purchases

Open either store from the title screen and choose Restore. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Purchases and billing

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Your local price appears in the game before you buy, and Apple provides the final confirmation.

Privacy choices

Where available, you can review advertising consent choices from the game’s Settings screen. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Remove Ads

This purchase removes automatic adverts between games and levels. You can still voluntarily watch rewarded adverts for bonus flights.

Additional game modes

The Web Store purchase unlocks Endless Classic, Limited Flights and Hazel modes. Once restored, choose a mode in Settings.
